🎧 Spotify Data Analysis Dashboard
A comprehensive, interactive dashboard for visualizing and analyzing personal Spotify listening data. This project transforms raw streaming history into insightful metrics, trends, and charts, helping users explore their favorite artists, songs, and listening habits over time.

✨ Features
This dashboard provides a deep dive into your listening data across multiple views:

🏠 Home/Overview Tab
Key Metrics: Quick summaries of your total Songs, Artists, Average Min listened per song, and Average Popularity.
Content Type Breakdown: Donut charts showing the distribution of your songs by album_type (single vs. album) and Explicit/Non-Explicit status.
Timeline Analysis: Line and bar charts illustrating Popularity by Month and Songs by Month, revealing listening trends throughout the year.
Top Content: Bar charts for Artist Songs and Songs by Artist showing the most frequently streamed content.

🎤 Artists Tab
Artist Demographics: Bar chart displaying the Distinct Artists by song.
Popularity Insights: Charts for Total Popularity by Song and Avg Hits by Artist (average popularity per year).
Detailed Artist Table: A dynamic table providing a comprehensive breakdown of each artist, including Songs, album_type count, Hits Song count, Avg Tracks Per Album, and Avg Popularity.

🎵 Songs Tab
Song Metrics: A sortable and filterable table listing individual songs with metrics like album_type, Month released/streamed, Avg Popularity, Min Popularity, Max Popularity, and Avg Duration (Minutes).

🛠️ Technology Stack
Platform/Visualization Tool: Microsoft Power BI Desktop
Data Preparation: Spreadsheets Microsoft Excel  for cleaning and initial ETL from raw Spotify data.
Data Source: Spotify Streaming History Data (obtained from Spotify's 'Request your personal data' feature).
Languages/Tools Used: Power Query (M language) for data transformation within Power BI, and Data Analysis Expressions (DAX) for creating measures and calculated columns.
